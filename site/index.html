    Meet the founder

    Bosco David

    Founder & choreographer

    Bosco is a choreographer from Mumbai. He started Bosco Dance Company in Hyderabad in 2006, and since then the team has done more than 3,000 shows, from family weddings to big corporate stages.

    He’s involved in every show. He works out the idea and the music with you, then rehearses with the dancers until the act is ready for the stage.

    Bosco also pays for medical insurance for his core dancers, and he started the BDC Foundation to support dancers across India.

        Services

        What we do

        Choreography and dancers for weddings and corporate events, plus props, lighting and music if you need them.

        How it works

        From first message to show day.

        This is how most bookings go.

        1. 01

          Message us

          Send us your date, venue and what you have in mind on WhatsApp.

        2. 02

          We plan the act

          Bosco and the team work out the idea, the music, the props and the lighting.

        3. 03

          Rehearsals

          Our dancers rehearse the act, and we teach the couple and family their steps.

        4. 04

          Show day

          The team comes in early, checks everything with your planner and DJ, and performs.
